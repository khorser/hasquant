 1581 -- bootstrapped curve cannot be asked what it was built from -- @PiecewiseYieldCurve@ is a
 1582 -- template with no instruments accessor, and an @Observer@ does not expose the observables it
 1583 -- registered with -- so a curve's fixing dependencies are the union of its helpers', and this
 1584 -- answers for one helper.  It walks the helper's underlying instrument the way
 1585 -- 'QuantLib.CashFlow.fixingDependencies' walks a leg, and names each index by
 1586 -- 'QuantLib.Index.name', the key QuantLib\'s process-global fixing store uses.
 1587 --
 1588 -- A swap, OIS, basis-swap, BMA, multiple-resets, cross-currency or bond helper answers from its
 1589 -- underlying; an IBOR futures or FX swap helper, and a deposit or FRA relative to the evaluation
 1590 -- date, reads no stored fixing: @Just []@. A 'DepositOnFixingDate' deposit, or a 'FraBetweenDates'
 1591 -- FRA with an indexed coupon, reads its fixing once the fixing date is before the evaluation date,
 1592 -- and reports it from then on; on the fixing date itself it still forecasts.
 1593 -- An overnight-index or SOFR futures helper reports each business-day fixing from its period start
 1594 -- up to the evaluation date, that date's included because the future reads it when stored. On
 1595 -- QuantLib 1.43, which keeps the future private, it instead reports 'Nothing' from two weeks before
 1596 -- its period starts. Any helper type the walk has no case for reports 'Nothing' too: "cannot see
