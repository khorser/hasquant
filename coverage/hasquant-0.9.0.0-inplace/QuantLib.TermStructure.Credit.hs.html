  148 -- | Apply a live hazard shift, retaining the underlying reference date, day counter,
  149 -- calendar and maximum date. The underlying and all quotes remain observed.
  150 --
  151 -- Parallel and relative shifts retain QuantLib's original wrappers and numerical
  152 -- integration; parallel queries additionally check finite nonnegative hazards.
  153 -- The shaped adapter is defined by hasquant: it adds the spread to
  154 -- the underlying hazard and multiplies underlying survival by
  155 -- @exp(-integral spread dt)@. QuantLib's linear/forward-flat interpolation primitives
  156 -- integrate the spread exactly, so bucket boundaries introduce no survival jumps
  157 -- and the accumulated change remains after the last bucket. The base curve is
  158 -- never sampled or re-interpolated. Curve times use its own day counter.
  159 --
  160 -- Shaped queries refuse non-finite spreads, negative or non-finite resulting hazards,
  161 -- and non-finite survival probabilities or values outside [0,1]. Multiplication
  162 -- roundoff at most four machine epsilons above one is clamped to one, so exactly
  163 -- zero hazards are not refused because @exp(-x) * exp(x)@ rounded upward.
  164 -- Survival queries
  165 -- check their endpoint and both sides of earlier spread knots; this is not a proof
  166 -- of positivity at every point of an arbitrary underlying curve. Underflow and
  167 -- overflow of the exponential correction remain native floating-point limits.
