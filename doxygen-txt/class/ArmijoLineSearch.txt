ArmijoLineSearch
 -- |Armijo line search.
 -- |Let $ \alpha $ and $ \beta $ be 2 scalars in $ [0,1] $. Let $ x $ be the current value of the unknown, $ d $ the search direction and $ t $ the step. Let $ f $ be the function to minimize. The line search stops when $ t $ verifies \[ f(x + t \cdot d) - f(x) \leq -\alpha t f'(x+t \cdot d) \] and \[ f(x+\frac{t}{\beta} \cdot d) - f(x) > -\frac{\alpha}{\beta} t f'(x+t \cdot d) \] (see Polak, Algorithms and consistent approximations, Optimization, volume 124 of Applied Mathematical Sciences, Springer-Verlag, NY, 1997)
 -f-
  -- |Default constructor.
  ArmijoLineSearch
   ->
   ::Real
   =1e-8
   -- ^eps
   ::Real
   =0.05
   -- ^alpha
   ::Real
   =0.65
   -- ^beta
