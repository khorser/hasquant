 -- |One-factor Student t - Gaussian Copula.
 -- |The copula model \[ Y_i = a_i\,M+\sqrt{1-a_i^2}\:Z_i \] is specified here by setting the probability density functions for $ Z_i $ ( $ D_Z $) to a Gaussian and for $ M $ ( $ D_M $) to a Student t-distribution with $ N_m $ degrees of freedom.The variance of the Student t-distribution with $ \nu $ degrees of freedom is $ \nu / (\nu - 2) $. Since the copula approach requires zero mean and unit variance distributions, $ M $ is scaled by $ \sqrt{(N_m - 2) / N_m}. $Possible enhancementsImprove performance/accuracy of the calculation of inverse cumulative Y.
