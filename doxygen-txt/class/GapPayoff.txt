GapPayoff
 -- |Binary gap payoff.
 -- |This payoff is equivalent to being a) long a PlainVanillaPayoff at the first strike (same Call/Put type) and b) short a CashOrNothingPayoff at the first strike (same Call/Put type) with cash payoff equal to the difference between the second and the first strike. Warningthis payoff can be negative depending on the strikes

  secondStrike
