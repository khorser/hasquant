CappedFlooredCoupon
 -- |Capped and/or floored floating-rate coupon.
 -- |The payoff $ P $ of a capped floating-rate coupon is: \[ P = N \times T \times \min(a L + b, C). \] The payoff of a floored floating-rate coupon is: \[ P = N \times T \times \max(a L + b, F). \] The payoff of a collared floating-rate coupon is: \[ P = N \times T \times \min(\max(a L + b, F), C). \] where \form#32 is the notional, \form#45 is the accrual time, \form#46 is the floating rate, \form#47 is its gearing, \form#48 is the spread, and \form#49 and \form#50 the strikes. They can be decomposed in the following manner. Decomposition of a capped floating rate coupon: \[ R = \min(a L + b, C) = (a L + b) + \min(C - b - \xi |a| L, 0) \] where $ \xi = sgn(a) $. Then: \[ R = (a L + b) + |a| \min(\frac{C - b}{|a|} - \xi L, 0) \]
