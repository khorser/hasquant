Calendar
 -- |calendar class
 -- |This class provides methods for determining whether a date is a business day or a holiday for a given market, and for incrementing/decrementing a date of a given number of business days.The Bridge pattern is used to provide the base behavior of the calendar, namely, to determine whether a date is a business day.A calendar should be defined for specific exchange holiday schedule or for general country holiday schedule. Legacy city holiday schedule calendars will be moved to the exchange/country convention.Teststhe methods for adding and removing holidays are tested by inspecting the calendar before and after their invocation.
