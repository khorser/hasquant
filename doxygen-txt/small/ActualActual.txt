 -- |Actual/Actual day count.
 -- |The day count can be calculated according to: - the ISDA convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Historical)", "Actual/Actual", "Act/Act", and according to ISDA also "Actual/365", "Act/365", and "A/365"; - the ISMA and US Treasury convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Bond)"; - the AFB convention, also known as "Actual/Actual (Euro)". For more details, refer to http://www.isda.org/publications/pdf/Day-Count-Fracation1999.pdf Teststhe correctness of the results is checked against known good values.
