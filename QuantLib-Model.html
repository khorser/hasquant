Price of a discount bond paying 1 at maturity, given the short rate rate at time now. Not pure: the model's short-rate fitting function depends on its YieldTermStructure handle, which can be relinked after construction, so the result at fixed arguments can change between two calls -- a genuine IO action, not a value fixed at construction time like the other {#fun pure ...#} bindings in this codebase.
