a smile section calibrated to a market smile (strikes/vols given directly, not as live quotes -- calibration runs once, eagerly, at construction). alpha/beta/nu/rho/vegaWeighted are the SABR calibration's initial guess and fixed/free flags; endCriteria/optimization method are left at QuantLib's own internal defaults (a raw, Haskell-finalized EndCriteria or OptimizationMethod handle can't safely be stored for this object's full lifetime -- see the qlXxxFitting comment in QuantLib.Internal.Enum for the same ownership hazard elsewhere).
